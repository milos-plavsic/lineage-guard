# Screenshot plan

Capture from the actual running dashboard at 1440 × 900 or larger. Do not use generated mockups.

1. **Hero image:** full dashboard showing the incident title, contained state, metrics, and all three
   downstream decisions. Use as the primary Devpost image.
2. **Recovery twin:** capture the rejected clamp, verified restoration, six checks, and certificate.
3. **Chronos trinity:** capture the Incident Genome, blocked/safe/expired change cards, coverage map,
   and in-toto passport.
4. **ProofGraph Trust Lens:** capture a selected decision, its Causal Cut, counterfactual result,
   Evidence Gap Radar, and visible unsigned-bundle disclosure.
5. **Evidence timeline:** crop containing the twelve stages from signal through proof and approval.
6. **Proof Pack:** show the graph, cuts, gap ranking, bundle, regression fixture, and manifest.
7. **DataHub proof:** live DataHub lineage view showing the raw, staging, billing, and demographics
   assets. Capture only from the remote verification environment and hide account-identifying data.
8. **Write-back proof:** source incident description and quarantine tag after approved live write-back.

Before upload, verify that screenshots contain no tokens, local usernames, browser profiles, private
URLs, unrelated tabs, notifications, or proprietary metadata. Use PNG, preserve readable text, and
add descriptive alt text in Devpost.
